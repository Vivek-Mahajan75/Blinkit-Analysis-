### **Project: Blinkit Sales Data Analysis**

* **Objective:** Conducted an in-depth analysis of Blinkit's sales data to derive actionable business insights such as identifying top-selling items, outlet performance, and sales trends by product type and location.

* **Tools & Technologies Used:**

  * **Python:** For data manipulation and analysis
  * **Libraries:** `pandas`, `numpy`, `matplotlib`, `seaborn` for data visualization and statistical exploration
  * **Jupyter Notebook:** For interactive analysis and reporting

* **Key Responsibilities & Achievements:**

  * Cleaned and preprocessed raw data including handling missing values and standardizing categorical variables.
  * Derived key KPIs: Total Sales, Average Sales, Number of Items Sold, and Average Customer Ratings.
  * Visualized data using bar plots, pie charts, and line graphs to show:

    * Sales distribution by item fat content and item type
    * Trends by outlet establishment year
    * Sales performance by outlet location type and size
  * Provided insights to support marketing and operational strategies, such as focusing on high-performing outlet types and item categories.

* **Impact:** Enabled better decision-making for inventory management and outlet planning based on data-driven evidence of sales patterns.
